Please fix the following:

    Your teacher sees this name. 2–80 characters.

    What this teacher can see

    Always shared: the name you gave this group and the date you joined.

    Never shared: your email, age, individual answers, mistake log, test scores and payments. Only results from the day you joined count.

    Parent or guardian consent

    Students under 18 need a parent or guardian to agree before results are shared with a teacher. Your account doesn’t show that you are 18 or older, so this step is required.
