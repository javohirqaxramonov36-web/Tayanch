Storage and Cookies

Draft dated: 2026-11-11.

Uzbek version (main text)

In short

  • Tayanch does not use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics cookies.
  • The site uses your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage. Keys start with tayanch.
  • This data stays in your browser. Only the sign-in session is used to reach the Supabase server.

What is stored

KeyWhyHow long
tayanch:authYour sign-in session (written by Supabase). Removed when you sign out.Until you clear it
tayanch:auth-code-verifierA one-time check code used while signing in with Google.Until you clear it
tayanch:hisob-keyinWhich page to return to after signing in with Google.Until you close the tab
tayanch:mavzu, tayanch:sidebarLight or dark theme and the side panel state.Until you clear it
tayanch:qavatWhich floor of the site you are on.Until you close the tab
tayanch:sat-…SAT: answers, lesson progress, mistake log, review, vocabulary, plan, official tests, question of the day, payment status, study preferences.Until you clear it
tayanch:sat-tarif-keshSAT: your current plan (for the Upgrade link in the side panel), kept for 10 minutes.Until you close the tab
tayanch:sat-reytingThe period (week or month) and view you chose on the SAT leaderboard.Until you clear it
tayanch:sat-sessiyaThe current SAT practice session.Until you close the tab
tayanch:ielts-…, tayanch:top-admission-…IELTS and Top Admission: lesson progress, attempts and plan.Until you clear it
tayanch:navigator-…, tayanch:keyingi-qadamYour answers on the home page road map and your next step.Until you clear it
tayanch:analitika-kirganWhether you have opened the results page before (a flag only).Until you clear it
tayanch:metrika:…When a usage statistics event was last sent (to avoid duplicate requests; a time only).Until you close the tab
tayanch-topshirish-yoliYour marks in the Application path tool.Until you clear it

How to clear it

  • Delete this site's data in your browser settings ("site data").
  • Signing out removes the session key. Your answers and results stay in the browser.
  • To delete server data, see the Privacy Policy.

Import this device's progress?

This browser has SAT progress saved from before you signed in. Import it to your account to keep it on all your devices. Nothing is sent until you choose Import.

    Progress imported

    Your progress is saved to your account and will sync across your devices. A copy also stays in this browser.

    Clear the copy in this browser?

    SAT progress stored in this browser will be removed. The copy in your account is not affected. While you are signed in, it is downloaded again when you open a SAT page.
