Import this device's progress?

This browser has SAT progress saved from before you signed in. Import it to your account to keep it on all your devices. Nothing is sent until you choose Import.

    Progress imported

    Your progress is saved to your account and will sync across your devices. A copy also stays in this browser.

    Clear the copy in this browser?

    SAT progress stored in this browser will be removed. The copy in your account is not affected. While you are signed in, it is downloaded again when you open a SAT page.
