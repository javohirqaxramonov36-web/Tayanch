Foundation lesson

Linear equations in one variable

Theory

An equation says that two expressions have the same value. To keep it true, perform the same operation on both sides. Undo addition with subtraction and multiplication with division by a nonzero number. After isolating the variable, substitute your result into the original equation to check it.

Nazariya (UZ)

Tenglamaning ikki tomoni teng qiymatga ega. Tenglikni saqlash uchun amalni ikkala tomonga ham bajaring. Noma’lumni yolg‘iz qoldirish uchun qo‘shilgan sonni ayiring, ko‘paytuvchiga bo‘ling. Bo‘luvchi nol bo‘lmasligi kerak. Topilgan javobni dastlabki tenglamaga qo‘yib tekshiring.

Examples

Example 1 — Undo operations

Solve 7x − 9 = 26. Add 9 to both sides to get 7x = 35. Divide both sides by 7 to get x = 5. Check: 7 × 5 − 9 equals 26.

Example 2 — Isolate a group

Solve 4(x + 3) = 36. First divide both sides by 4 to get x + 3 = 9, then subtract 3. The solution is x = 6.
