SAT® timing and retakes

When to start, which official rules matter for a retake, and a checklist to go through before you register again.

No score is guaranteed. Rules and dates are copied from College Board pages with the date we checked them. They can change: always confirm on the official page before you register.

Official rules

  • Official · CB32

    Score Choice lets you choose which scores to send, by test date. Some colleges and scholarship programs require you to send all your scores.

  • Official · CB33

    Each time you register for a weekend SAT, you can send your score to up to four organizations for free. You can use or change these free sends until nine days after the test; after that there is a fee.

  • Official · CB32

    Score Choice does not apply to the free score sends you pick when you register.

  • Official · CB35

    Most scores from weekend tests are released 2–4 weeks after test day.

  • Official · CB34

    The same test dates and deadlines apply to US and international students. Deadlines end at 11:59 p.m. ET (US Eastern time). Late registration is available worldwide for an extra fee.

When to retake

  • Official · CB31

    Students can take the SAT as many times as they want.

  • Official · CB31

    College Board recommends taking it at least twice: in the spring of junior year and the fall of senior year (the US school calendar).
