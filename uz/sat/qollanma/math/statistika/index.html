Statistics: the mean runs, the median stays

The points sit on a number line. Drag one of them far away: the mean follows it, while the median stays almost where it was. The standard deviation shows how spread out the points are.

After this lesson

  • You quickly calculate the mean, median and range of a list.
  • You can tell which measure an outlier changes the most.
  • You compare the spread of two data sets without calculating the standard deviation.

Sample data

Drag a point, or select it with Tab and move it with the arrow keys. Blue line and triangle: mean. Black dashed line and diamond: median. Gray area with dashed edges: mean ± standard deviation.

The largest point slowly moves to 20. Watch: the mean (blue) follows it, the median (black) stays in place, and the gray area widens.

Exercises

Sample questions written for Tayanch, not official SAT questions. No score is calculated.

Exercise 1

SampleeasyMeannot verified

What is the mean of the numbers 4, 7, 7, 9, 13?

Solution. Sum: 4 + 7 + 7 + 9 + 13 = 40. Mean: 40 ÷ 5 = 8.

Exercise 2

SampleeasyMediannot verified

What is the median of the numbers 3, 9, 4, 18, 6, 8?

Solution. Sort: 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 18. The count is even, so the two middle numbers are 6 and 8. Median: (6 + 8) ÷ 2 = 7.

Exercise 3

SamplemediumChange in the meannot verified

The mean of 10 numbers is 12. The number 23 is added to the list. What is the new mean?

Solution. Old sum: 10 · 12 = 120. New sum: 120 + 23 = 143, and the count is 11. 143 ÷ 11 = 13.

Exercise 4

SamplemediumOutliernot verified

The number 40 is added to the list 5, 6, 6, 7, 8. Which of the following changes the least?

Solution. The median changes from 6 to 6.5. The mean rises from 6.4 to 12, the range from 3 to 35, and the standard deviation also increases sharply.

Exercise 5

SamplehardStandard deviationnot verified

Set A: 10, 10, 10, 10. Set B: 7, 9, 11, 13. Which statement is true?

Solution. Both means are 10. In A, every number equals the mean: the standard deviation is 0. In B, the numbers are 1 and 3 away from 10: the standard deviation is √5 ≈ 2.24.

Summary

  • Mean = sum ÷ count. A single large value pulls it toward itself.
  • Median: the middle of the sorted list; if the count is even, the mean of the two middle numbers.
  • Range = largest − smallest. An outlier changes the range right away.
  • Standard deviation: how far the values are from the mean on average. If all values are equal, it is 0.
