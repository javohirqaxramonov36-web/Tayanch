Graphs: what an equation draws

Every number controls something on the graph: m controls the slope, b where the line crosses, h and k the vertex of the parabola. Move a slider or drag a point, and the equation changes right away.

After this lesson

  • You can read m and b in y = mx + b from a graph.
  • You find the vertex, the axis of symmetry and the roots from the three forms of a parabola.
  • You tell apart an intersection of two equations, no solution and infinitely many solutions.

Exercises

Sample questions written for Tayanch, not official SAT questions. No score is calculated.

Exercise 1

SampleeasyStraight linenot verified

At what point does the line y = 2x − 3 intersect the y-axis?

Solution. On the y-axis, x = 0. y = 2 · 0 − 3 = −3. The point is (0, −3).

Exercise 2

SamplemediumIntersection of two linesnot verified

At what point do the lines y = 2x + 1 and y = −x + 7 intersect?

Solution. 2x + 1 = −x + 7 → 3x = 6 → x = 2. y = 2 · 2 + 1 = 5. Check: −2 + 7 = 5.

Exercise 3

SamplemediumParabola: axis of symmetrynot verified

What is the axis of symmetry of the parabola f(x) = 2(x − 3)² − 8?

Solution. In the form a(x − h)² + k, the vertex is (h, k) = (3, −8). The axis of symmetry passes through the vertex: x = 3.

Exercise 4

SamplehardParabola: minimum valuenot verified

What is the minimum value of the function f(x) = x² − 6x + 5?

Solution. Vertex: x = −b / (2a) = 6 / 2 = 3. f(3) = 9 − 18 + 5 = −4. Another way: x² − 6x + 5 = (x − 3)² − 4, so the minimum value is −4.

Exercise 5

SamplehardSystem of equationsnot verified

How many solutions does the system of equations y = 3x + 2 and y = 3x − 4 have?

Solution. The slopes are equal (3), but the y-intercepts are different (2 and −4): the lines are parallel and never intersect. 3x + 2 = 3x − 4 → 2 = −4, which is false.

Summary

  • y = mx + b: m is the slope (how much y changes when x increases by 1), and b gives the y-intercept (0, b).
  • a(x − h)² + k: the vertex is (h, k), and the axis of symmetry is x = h. If a > 0 it opens upward, if a < 0 it opens downward.
  • In ax² + bx + c, the vertex is at x = −b / (2a), and the y-intercept is (0, c).
  • Equal slopes, different b: the lines are parallel and there is no solution. Equal slopes and equal b: the same line, infinitely many solutions.
