Sample

Question viewer

A preview of the component that shows SAT® practice questions: Reading and Writing and Math, multiple choice and student-produced response. The questions were written for Tayanch as samples. They are not official SAT questions. Nothing on this page is saved or sent anywhere.

Mode

Event log

The events the component sends to the page, newest first.

    Keyboard

    • A–D: choose an answer (when focus is inside the question)
    • ↑ ↓: move between answer choices and choose
    • Space or Enter: choose the focused answer
    • Enter in the answer box: submit a student-produced response
