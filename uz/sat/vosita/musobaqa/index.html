        How contests work

        • Each contest has a window. You can start any time inside it, once. Your timer starts when you press Start and stops at the time limit or when the window closes, whichever comes first.
        • Each answer is final once you save it. You do not see whether it is right until the contest ends.
        • Your score is the number of correct answers. Equal scores are ordered by time, from your start to your last saved answer. Equal score and time share a rank.
        • The results table shows nicknames only, and only for accounts marked 18 or older that joined the leaderboard. Everyone who took part sees their own score.
        • Contest answers do not use your plan's daily limits and do not give XP. Questions are original and checked by a Tayanch author. There are no prizes.
