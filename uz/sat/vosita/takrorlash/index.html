Mistake log: review

Questions you got wrong in the lessons and the question bank land here and come back at intervals: tomorrow, then after 3, 7 and 14 days. Solve one correctly and it moves to the next box; get it wrong and it goes back to the start. Note why you missed it, so you fix the cause, not just the answer.

Sample questions written for Tayanch, not official SAT questions. No score is calculated. The intervals are Tayanch's choice.
