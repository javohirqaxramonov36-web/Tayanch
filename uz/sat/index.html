    Where the SAT counts

    Four examples. Each university sets its own rules.

    • Harvard CollegeUSARequired

      The SAT or ACT is required, for international applicants too. Only if neither test is accessible does Harvard accept other results, such as A-Levels, IB or a national exam.

      Official page · checked 2026-11-09

    • NYU Abu DhabiUAEOptional

      Test-optional through the 2027–2028 application cycle. Scores you choose to send are still reviewed.

      Official page · checked 2026-11-09

    • METUTürkiyeAccepted

      The SAT is one of the exams you can apply with, alongside TR-YÖS and others. Scores are valid for two years.

      Official page · checked 2026-11-09

    • Kyoto University iUPJapanAccepted

      At least one standardized test is mandatory, and the SAT is on the list. For October 2027 entry, only tests taken by November 7, 2026 count. Engineering also needs physics and chemistry results, which the SAT does not cover.

      Official page · checked 2026-11-09

    Rules change every cycle. Check the official page before you apply.
