Mock test

A full-length, timed practice test with the same number of modules, questions and minutes as the digital SAT®. Every question is an original written and checked by the author.

Checking your mock tests…

ResumeSign in

The timer starts only when you start a module. You can leave between modules, but once a module starts its timer keeps running, even if you close the page.

How the mock test works

PartQuestionsTime
Reading and Writing · Module 127 questions32 minutes
Reading and Writing · Module 227 questions32 minutes
Break10 minutes
Math · Module 122 questions35 minutes
Math · Module 222 questions35 minutes
  • In total: 98 questions and 2 hours 14 minutes of testing time, plus the break.
  • Not adaptive: the second module of each section has the same mix of easy, medium and hard questions as the first. On the real test, your first module decides how hard the second one is.
  • No unscored questions: on the real test each module has 2 pretest questions that don't count. Here every question counts.
  • You can change answers and mark questions for review until you submit the module. You can't go back to a module after it ends.
  • When a module's time runs out, it's submitted with the answers you saved.
  • Your score is a rough estimate from a simple formula, not an official SAT score. Mistakes go to your redo list.
