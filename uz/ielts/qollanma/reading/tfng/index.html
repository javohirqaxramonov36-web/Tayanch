Reading darsiIdentifying informationQoralama

True, False yoki Not Given: dalil gapdan boshlang

Bu savolda ko'pchilik False va Not Given ni chalkashtiradi. Farqi bitta: False uchun matnda zid gap bo'lishi shart. Shuning uchun avval dalil gapni topasiz, javobni keyin tanlaysiz.

Bu darsdan keyin

  • Da'voning har qismini matndagi aniq gap bilan solishtirasiz.
  • False (matn zid) va Not Given (matnda yo'q) ni dalil bo'yicha ajratasiz.
  • Parafrazni taniysiz: boshqa so'zlar bilan aytilgan bir xil ma'no.

0 / 5 mashq to'g'ri yechilgan

Laboratoriya

Namuna O'ylab topilgan vaziyat

1-da'voEvery family has to help with work that is shared.

Matnda bu da'voga tegadigan gap yo'q.

Bitta parcha, uch da'vo. Da'voni tanlang: sahnada dalil gap ko'k chegara bilan belgilanadi, yonida munosabati yoziladi.

Nega3-gap: "they must help with shared jobs". So'zlar boshqa, ma'no bir xil.
True
Matndagi gap da'vo bilan bir xil ma'noni aytadi, so'zlari boshqa bo'lsa ham.
False
Matndagi gap da'voga zid. Bunday gapni ko'rsata olishingiz kerak.
Not Given
Matnda da'voni tasdiqlaydigan ham, inkor qiladigan ham gap yo'q.
Tekshirish savoli
"Qaysi gap?" Javob bera olmasangiz, False emas, Not Given.

1 / 3. Avval sahnadagi dalil gapni bosing yoki "Bunday gap yo'q" ni tanlang. Keyin javobni tanlab, tekshiring.

Hali tanlanmagan.

Mashqlar

Tayanch uchun yozilgan namuna savollar, rasmiy IELTS savoli emas. Ball hisoblanmaydi.

1-mashq

NamunaosonParafraz: boshqa so'zlar bilantekshirilmagan

Parchani o'qing. Da'vo matndagi ma'lumotga mos kelsa True, zid bo'lsa False, matnda bu haqda ma'lumot bo'lmasa Not Given.

The hill town museum was founded by a group of retired teachers. For its first ten years, it opened only at weekends. When the town gave it a larger building, it began opening every day except Monday.

Statement: The museum is now closed on one day each week.

Yechim. Oxirgi gap: muzey dushanbadan boshqa har kuni ochiq. Demak haftada bir kun yopiq. So'zlar boshqa, ma'no bir xil: True.

2-mashq

Namunao'rtaEski holat va hozirgi holattekshirilmagan

Parchani o'qing. Da'vo matndagi ma'lumotga mos kelsa True, zid bo'lsa False, matnda bu haqda ma'lumot bo'lmasa Not Given.

A student radio station at a riverside college began broadcasting fifteen years ago. At first, all of its programmes were music shows. Today, more than half of its weekly hours are filled with news and interviews made by students.

Statement: Music still takes up most of the station's weekly broadcasting time.

Yechim. Hozir vaqtning yarmidan ko'pi yangilik va intervyuga ketadi, ya'ni musiqaga yarmidan kami qoladi. Da'vo "musiqa ko'pchilik" deydi, matn buning teskarisini aytadi: False.

3-mashq

Namunao'rtaMatnda yo'q ma'lumottekshirilmagan

Parchani o'qing. Da'vo matndagi ma'lumotga mos kelsa True, zid bo'lsa False, matnda bu haqda ma'lumot bo'lmasa Not Given.

A city's first bicycle-sharing scheme started with two hundred bicycles at twenty stations. Users pay a small fee through a mobile app and can return a bicycle to any station. The scheme proved so popular that the number of stations was doubled within two years.

Statement: Most users of the scheme are university students.

Yechim. Matn sxema qanday ishlashi va mashhur bo'lgani haqida. Foydalanuvchilar kimligi (talabami yoki yo'q) haqida na tasdiq, na inkor bor: Not Given.

4-mashq

NamunaqiyinYaqin so'z tuzog'itekshirilmagan

Parchani o'qing. Da'vo matndagi ma'lumotga mos kelsa True, zid bo'lsa False, matnda bu haqda ma'lumot bo'lmasa Not Given.

The bakery on Market Street uses flour from a mill in the next valley. The owner says that the mill grinds its wheat slowly, which keeps more of the grain's flavour. The bakery's bread costs more than bread sold in the supermarket.

Statement: The bakery's bread is more popular than supermarket bread.

Yechim. Matnda nonning narxi solishtirilgan (qimmatroq), mashhurligi emas. Qancha odam sotib olishi haqida hech narsa yo'q: Not Given.

5-mashq

NamunaqiyinVaqt tartibitekshirilmagan

Parchani o'qing. Da'vo matndagi ma'lumotga mos kelsa True, zid bo'lsa False, matnda bu haqda ma'lumot bo'lmasa Not Given.

A writer from a small coastal town spent six years on her first novel. A small publisher accepted the book, and it sold slowly at first. In its second year, a radio interview with the writer brought it many new readers.

Statement: The novel was popular as soon as it was published.

Yechim. "Sold slowly at first": kitob boshida sekin sotilgan. Da'vo esa chiqishi bilan mashhur bo'lgan deydi. Matn teskarisini aytadi: False.

Xulosa

  • Avval da'voni o'qing va uning kalit qismini ajrating: kim, nima, qachon, qancha.
  • Matnda shu qismga tegadigan gapni toping. U boshqa so'zlar bilan yozilgan bo'lishi mumkin.
  • Gap da'voni tasdiqlasa True, teskarisini aytsa False.
  • Zid gap topilmasa, javob Not Given. Umumiy bilim va taxmin hisob emas.
  • "At first", "today", "more than" kabi so'zlarga e'tibor bering: ular ma'noni o'zgartiradi.
