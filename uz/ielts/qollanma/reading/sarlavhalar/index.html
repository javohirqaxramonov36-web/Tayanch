Matching headings: paragraf nima haqida

Sarlavha paragrafdagi bitta detalni emas, butun asosiy fikrni umumlashtiradi. Sarlavhalar paragraflardan ko'p, shuning uchun bittasi ortiqcha qoladi. Paragrafni tanlang, keyin unga mos sarlavhani bosing.

Bu darsdan keyin

  • Paragrafning asosiy fikrini qo'llab-quvvatlovchi detallardan ajratasiz.
  • Matndagi so'z sarlavhada takrorlangani uchun uni tanlamaysiz.
  • Juda keng yoki matnda yo'q fikrli sarlavhani chetga surasiz.

Laboratoriya

Namuna O'ylab topilgan matn

Sarlavhalar (bittasi ortiqcha)

A paragraf namuna: imtihonda ham ba'zan bitta paragraf oldindan moslangan bo'ladi. Sahnada asosiy fikr gapi tagchiziq bilan, mos sarlavha ko'k fon bilan belgilangan.

NegaBirinchi gap asosiy fikr: butun shahar soatga qarab yashagan. Ikkinchi gap buni misollar bilan ko'rsatadi.
Asosiy fikr
Paragrafning hamma gapi shu fikrga xizmat qiladi. Sarlavha uni qamraydi.
Detal
Bitta misol, son yoki tafsilot. U asosiy fikrni ko'rsatadi, lekin o'zi sarlavha emas.
Kalit so'z tuzog'i
Paragrafdagi so'z sarlavhada bor, lekin ma'no boshqa.
Juda keng
Mavzu umumiy, paragraf esa uning bitta holati haqida.

Vazifa. A paragraf namuna. B va C uchun: sahnada paragrafni bosing, keyin unga mos sarlavhani bosing. Bitta sarlavha faqat bitta paragrafga beriladi. Hammasi tayyor bo'lsa, tekshiring.

Hali biriktirilmagan.

Mashqlar

Tayanch uchun yozilgan namuna savollar, rasmiy IELTS savoli emas. Ball hisoblanmaydi.

1-mashq

NamunaosonAsosiy fikr va kirishtekshirilmagan

Paragrafni o'qing va uning asosiy fikrini eng yaxshi umumlashtiradigan sarlavhani tanlang.

Many small towns have lost their weekly markets as large supermarkets opened nearby. In one hill town, however, the market has grown. Farmers now sell there every Saturday, and the town has added a covered area so that the market can continue in winter. Local people say it is busier than it has been for decades.

Which heading best summarises the main idea of the paragraph?

Yechim. Birinchi gap umumiy holatni beradi, "however" dan keyin asosiy fikr keladi: shu shaharda bozor aksincha o'sgan. Qolgan gaplar shuni misollar bilan ko'rsatadi.

2-mashq

Namunao'rtaIjobiy va salbiy natijatekshirilmagan

Paragrafni o'qing va uning asosiy fikrini eng yaxshi umumlashtiradigan sarlavhani tanlang.

A city school tried starting lessons an hour later for one term. Teachers recorded fewer students arriving late, and many students said they felt less tired in morning classes. However, some parents found the new timetable difficult because it no longer matched their working hours.

Which heading best summarises the main idea of the paragraph?

Yechim. Paragrafda ikki tomon bor: o'quvchilar uchun foyda va ota-onalar uchun qiyinchilik. Ikkalasini qamraydigan sarlavha: "aralash natija".

3-mashq

Namunao'rtaMuammo va yechimtekshirilmagan

Paragrafni o'qing va uning asosiy fikrini eng yaxshi umumlashtiradigan sarlavhani tanlang.

When the neighbourhood first put out recycling bins, very little was collected, because people were unsure what could go in them. The council then printed simple pictures on each bin showing which items belonged inside. Within months, the amount of correctly sorted waste had risen sharply.

Which heading best summarises the main idea of the paragraph?

Yechim. Tuzilish: muammo (nimani tashlashni bilmaslik), yechim (rasmlar), natija (to'g'ri saralash oshdi). Uchala qismni qamraydigan sarlavha faqat bitta.

4-mashq

NamunaqiyinRad etilgan qarashtekshirilmagan

Paragrafni o'qing va uning asosiy fikrini eng yaxshi umumlashtiradigan sarlavhani tanlang.

Translators are often imagined as people who simply replace each word with its equal in another language. In practice, the work involves constant choices. A joke may have to be replaced by a different joke, and a polite phrase in one culture may sound cold in another. Good translators therefore think about the effect on the reader as much as the meaning of the words.

Which heading best summarises the main idea of the paragraph?

Yechim. Birinchi gap keng tarqalgan qarash, "In practice" uni rad etadi. Asosiy fikr: tarjima tanlovlar ketma-ketligi, va oxirgi gap uni o'quvchiga ta'sir bilan bog'laydi.

5-mashq

NamunaqiyinO'zgarish: oldin va hozirtekshirilmagan

Paragrafni o'qing va uning asosiy fikrini eng yaxshi umumlashtiradigan sarlavhani tanlang.

For years, the old railway bridge was seen as an ugly reminder of the town's lost industry, and several plans to pull it down were discussed. A group of residents argued that it could be useful instead. Today it carries a footpath and a cycle lane, and it has become one of the most photographed places in the town.

Which heading best summarises the main idea of the paragraph?

Yechim. Paragraf o'zgarish haqida: oldin keraksiz deb ko'rilgan ko'prik endi qadrli joy. Faqat shu sarlavha boshini ham, oxirini ham qamraydi.

Xulosa

  • Sarlavha paragrafning hamma qismini qamraydi: boshini ham, oxirini ham.
  • Misol va detal asosiy fikr emas: ular asosiy fikrni ko'rsatadi.
  • "However", "in practice" kabi so'zdan keyin ko'pincha asosiy fikr keladi.
  • Paragrafdagi so'z sarlavhada bor bo'lsa, bu tuzoq bo'lishi mumkin: ma'noni tekshiring.
  • Ishonchli juftlardan boshlang; qolgan sarlavhalar soni kamayadi.
