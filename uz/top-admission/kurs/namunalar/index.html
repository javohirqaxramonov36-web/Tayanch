Yozuv namunalari

Quyidagi matnlar Tayanch uchun yaratilgan o‘quv namunalari. Ular haqiqiy talabaning hayoti yoki qabul natijasini tasvirlamaydi. Qavsli joylar tekshiriladigan shaxsiy dalil bilan to‘ldiriladi. Har namunadan keyin tuzilishini ko‘ring va o‘z tajribangiz bilan yangi matn yozing.

Namunani nusxalab topshirmang: tuzilishini o'rganing va o'z tajribangiz bilan yangi matn yozing. Ish shablonlari · Barcha darslar

Personal essay

Namuna

The first version of our project looked convincing on my screen. It produced an answer, displayed a chart, and gave me something I could explain quickly. When a teammate asked what would happen if the input were incomplete, I opened a different file instead of answering.

I had spent more time preparing the demonstration than understanding the failure. That afternoon, I began a separate notebook for cases that made the system behave badly. Some entries were mundane. Others showed assumptions I had never written down. The notebook gradually became more useful than the slides.

At our next meeting, I brought an example that failed. Explaining it felt uncomfortable. I could no longer present myself as the person who had solved the problem. But the conversation changed. A teammate questioned how we had prepared the data. Another suggested a simpler comparison. We began making decisions together instead of dividing the work into pieces that only one person understood.

I now keep a place for uncertainty in a project before I choose how to present it. I document what I tried, what I could observe, and what still needs an explanation. This habit also changed how I ask for help. A clear account of a failed attempt gives another person something to work with.

I still enjoy seeing a system run. The more lasting satisfaction comes when I can explain why I trust a result, where that trust ends, and which question should come next.

O'zbekcha

Loyihamizning birinchi versiyasi ekranimda ishonchli ko‘rinardi. U javob chiqardi, grafik ko‘rsatdi va menga tez tushuntirish mumkin bo‘lgan natija berdi. Jamoadoshim kirish ma’lumoti to‘liq bo‘lmasa nima bo‘lishini so‘raganda, javob berish o‘rniga boshqa faylni ochdim.

Men nosozlikni tushunishdan ko‘ra namoyishni tayyorlashga ko‘proq vaqt sarflagandim. O‘sha kuni tizim noto‘g‘ri ishlaydigan holatlar uchun alohida daftar boshladim. Ayrim yozuvlar oddiy edi. Boshqalari esa ilgari yozib qo‘ymagan taxminlarimni ko‘rsatdi. Asta-sekin daftar slaydlardan foydaliroq bo‘lib qoldi.

Keyingi uchrashuvga ishlamagan misol olib bordim. Uni tushuntirish noqulay edi. Endi o‘zimni muammoni hal qilgan odam sifatida ko‘rsata olmasdim. Ammo suhbat o‘zgardi. Bir jamoadoshimiz ma’lumotni qanday tayyorlaganimizni savol ostiga qo‘ydi. Boshqasi sodda taqqoslashni taklif qildi. Har kim faqat o‘z bo‘lagini tushunadigan ish o‘rniga qarorlarni birga qabul qila boshladik.

Endi loyihani qanday ko‘rsatishni tanlashdan avval undagi noaniqlik uchun joy ajrataman. Nimani sinaganimni, nimani kuzatganimni va yana nima izoh talab qilishini yozaman. Bu odat yordam so‘rashimni ham o‘zgartirdi. Muvaffaqiyatsiz urinishning aniq tavsifi boshqa odamga ham ishlash uchun asos beradi.

Tizim ishlaganini ko‘rish hanuz yoqadi. Natijaga nega ishonishimni, bu ishonchning chegarasini va keyingi savolni tushuntira olganimdagi qoniqish esa uzoqroq qoladi.

Tahlil. Sahnada kichik qaror bor, o‘rtada shaxsiy harakat va hamkorlik, oxirida esa keyingi ishga ta’sir ko‘rinadi. Profilingizga tegishli bo‘lmagan voqeani o‘zingizniki sifatida ishlatmang.
